Frequently asked questions

Is Blokido free?

Yes, Blokido is completely free. If you like, you can watch optional rewarded ads for extra crystals.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The whole game works offline; only the world leaderboard needs an internet connection.

Which languages are supported?

Blokido is available in 16 languages: Türkçe, Kurdî (Kurmancî), English, Deutsch, 中文（简体）, हिन्दी, தமிழ், Español, العربية, فارسی, Français, Italiano, বাংলা, Português (Brasil), Bahasa Indonesia, Русский.
