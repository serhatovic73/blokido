Summary

Blokido needs no account and never asks for your name, email, phone number or location. Your records, settings and crystals are stored on your device.

Stored on your device

Settings, records, mission progress, achievements, crystals, unlocked themes and an unfinished game are kept on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Leaderboard

If you use the leaderboard, a random device-specific ID, the nickname you choose and your scores are stored on Google Firebase servers. Your real name and contact details are never sent.

Ads

The game has optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may process data such as your advertising ID, IP address and device information: policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your phone's settings.

Children

Blokido does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the current version is published in the game. Questions: serhatgicer49@gmail.com
